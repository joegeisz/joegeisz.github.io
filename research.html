I am currently a graduate student at Colorado State Univerisity in the mathematics department. In my research I like to use tools from geometry and topology in new and applied ways. Applications I have explored include machine learning, radar, fractals, dynamical systems, and polymer physics.

USGS Research

From 2020-2024 I worked with the USGS Great Lakes Science Center in Ann Arbor MI, and the Great Lakes Research Center at Michigan Tech. I used machine learning and other mathematical techniques to analyze benthic imagery and other datasets related to the great lakes.
