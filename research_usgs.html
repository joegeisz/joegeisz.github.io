Joseph Geisz: USGS Research

USGS Great Lakes Science Center / Michigan Tech Great Lakes Research Center

From 2020-2024 I worked with the USGS Great Lakes Science Center in Ann Arbor MI, and the Great Lakes Research Center at Michigan Tech. I worked with Dr. Peter Esselman and his group at the GLSC to learn about the benthic ecosystem of the Great Lakes, in particular invasive species. We used autonomous underwater vehicles and other advanced technology to collect high quality datasets which we then analyzed with machine learning methods.

  • Papers:
    • Classification of Lakebed Geologic Substrate in Autonomously Collected Benthic Imagery Using Machine Learning
    • A transferable approach for quantifying benthic fish sizes and densities in annotated underwater images
  • Datasets:
    • Annotated images of dreissenid mussels (Dreissena spp.) in down-looking color images in Lake Michigan, USA, to support machine learning modeling
    • Assessment of Submerged Aquatic Vegetation in Lake Michigan Using Down-Looking AUV-Collected Imagery
    • Autonomously Collected Benthic Imagery for Substrate Prediction, Lake Michigan 2020-2021
    • Down-looking Images of Lake Trout (Salvelinus namaycush) and Round Goby (Neogobius melanostomus) to Support Automated Biomass Estimation
  • Code:
    • Benthic Imagery Labeling GUI
